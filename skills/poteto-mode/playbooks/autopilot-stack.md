### Autopilot-stack

**You own the stack, never the landing. Build and verify one linear chain of base-chained PRs, then hand it to the operator to review and land.** Use for "autopilot-stack", "stack them, don't ship", or an equivalent explicit request. [Autopilot-full](autopilot-full.md) holds except for how verified work leaves. Each audit re-reads both playbooks, and this one wins where they differ. Nothing auto-merges.

1. **Load the runtime and hold the boundary.** Resolve delegation, waiting, scheduling, control, and current-task history through **bstack-runtime**. Record the durable objective, repository, branches, and authorized publication operations. Merge, merge-when-ready, and deploy remain forbidden. A request for a plan alone does not start execution.
2. **Run Autopilot-full's owners, minus the landing.** Each owner follows Autopilot-full steps 2 through 4 for its stack unit, with its intended parent in the brief. Owners report STACK-READY in place of merge-ready, only with all required receipts and current-head checks. They never rebase onto another branch, trunk included, and never retarget, merge, close a PR, or arm auto-merge. The root owns the chain's topology and the operator owns the landing. When an item depends on another, its owner branches from that item's branch, not from trunk. Nothing enters the chain without a clean verdict from Autopilot-full step 4.
3. **Give the topology one writer.** Only the root appends, rebases, restacks, or changes a PR base, because two writers on one chain undo each other. Owners write only their assigned branch within the grant and report its tip, current base, and intended parent. Append each clean unit in dependency order, or in the operator's order, with its PR base set to its parent's branch. Only the root PR targets trunk. Use Graphite only when the repository already uses it or the operator requested it. Otherwise build a base-branch chain per [Opening a PR](opening-a-pr.md). Force-push only when explicitly authorized, after a current remote-SHA check, with a lease pinned to that SHA. Do not rewrite a shared branch. An owner takes the root's push as its new base and lease. On a conflict it resolves its own slice for the root to push.
4. **Absorb drift at the root.** Until landing starts, apply [Autopilot-full step 5](autopilot-full.md)'s merge-prep test to each link and rebase the chain bottom-up where it calls for one. Then apply [Shipping's verification reuse rule](shipping.md#verification-reuse) link by link, since a link's base is its parent. Re-run mergeability and CI at every rewritten head, including when the patch-id is unchanged. Send invalidated lanes back through Autopilot-full step 4 before delivery. Preserve operator gates and the approval boundaries in Autopilot-full step 6.
5. **Audit hourly through the runtime.** Follow [Autopilot-full step 6](autopilot-full.md) for scheduling, probes down to leaf lanes, stuck recovery, and catching the operator up per **ketchup** after an audit that changed something. Without scheduling, checkpoint locally and report the monitoring gap. Do not leave children running after the queue ends.
6. **Deliver the chain, then keep your hands off it.** Provide one ordered chain of verified PRs or local branches for bottom-up review. Each link carries its verdict in its PR when comment-writing is authorized, and in the report otherwise. The operator lands it. Withhold STACK-READY for gaps or unresolved findings. Once landing starts, nobody writes to the chain except the post-merge retarget that [Shipping](shipping.md) sanctions, because a second writer breaks a landing in flight. A mid-landing problem goes through the root to the operator, and any fix waits for the operator's go.
7. **Honor stop immediately.** A hold or stop sends every owner and child a zero-writes instruction and cancels future scheduled work.

**Choosing between the autopilots.** Use Autopilot-full only when independent PRs and explicit merge authority are both present. Use Autopilot-stack when the operator wants review before landing, the work is sequenced or coupled, or merge authority is withheld.

**Reply:** catch the operator up per **ketchup**, with links to the chain root and tip, a one-line verdict per link, trail locations, and anything parked or excluded with the reason.
